The problem

Advice is cheap. Follow-through is never checked.

Most tools will tell you what to do next. Almost none of them know whether you actually did it — they take your word for it, or they don't ask at all. VISION asks for real evidence before it counts.

How it works today

Real evidence, checked by the system — not self-reported

Depending on the task, VISION asks for a photo, a voice note, a live session, or a timed focus session. The system reviews what you submit and decides whether it counts. You can't mark your own work done.
