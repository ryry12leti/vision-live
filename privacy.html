Privacy

Last updated 13 July 2026

VISION is in early beta. This explains what we collect, how proof photos are handled, and the controls you have. We keep it short and honest rather than legally exhaustive.

What we collect

  • Account — your email and a hashed password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). We never see your raw password.
  • Onboarding answers — the goal, available time, blockers, and preferences you enter so the strategist can build your plan.
  • Proof & progress — the photos you upload as proof, plus the tasks, points, streaks, and Standing derived from them.
  • Feedback — anything you submit on the feedback form, so we can improve the product.

Connected accounts

You can optionally connect external apps on the Connections page so VISION can plan around your real life. Connections are always optional, read-only, and you can disconnect any of them at any time — the app works fully without them.

  • Which apps — right now: Google Calendar (free/busy) and Gmail (headers only). Coming later: Apple Health, Health Connect (Android), and fitness apps like Strava — these will need our mobile app.
  • What we read — the minimum needed: Calendar tells us when you are busy or free (never event titles or who you meet); Gmail tells us that a message was sent, when, and to which domain (never the subject, body, or attachments).
  • What we store — only short, summarised facts, e.g. "30 free minutes at 6:30pm", "sent 3 outreach emails in 2 days". These help set the best time and length for your tasks and remind you to follow up.
  • What we never store — your calendar event details, your email subjects or bodies, or any raw health streams. We never store your access tokens where the app or anyone else can read them — they are encrypted in a secure vault and only used by our server.
  • How we use it — to personalise your tasks and your Analyst's advice. We never sell it, never use it for ads, and never use a connection to fake proof or mark a task complete — proof is always your own photo/recording.
  • Health data — if you later connect fitness data, it is only used to adapt task difficulty and avoid overtraining. VISION never makes a medical diagnosis from it.
  • Disconnecting — press Disconnect on the Connections page. We revoke access at the provider where possible, delete the stored tokens, and remove the facts we derived from that app.

Your proof photos are private

Proof photos are uploaded to a private storage bucket. Access is owner-only — only your authenticated account can read your own files. They are not public, not shared to a feed, and not visible to other members. Friends in your circle see your effort and proof status (that you logged proof, your points and streak) — never the photo itself.

How points and Standing work

Points are awarded only on the server, inside a verified proof submission, and only when a real photo is attached — no photo, no points. Duplicate submissions of the same task earn nothing. Your Standing is an estimate of your own consistency based on your proof history, streak, and progress — it is not a live global ranking of other people.

What we do not do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't run ads or ad-tracking.
  • We don't make your proof photos public.
  • We don't provide medical, financial, legal, or mental-health advice — VISION is a self-improvement tool, not a professional service.

Data deletion & export

You can clear your local cache any time from Settings. To delete or export your account and stored data, email m.dhindsa787@gmail.com and we'll action it. As a beta, expect this to be handled manually. You can also reach the team any time through the feedback form in Settings.

Beta disclaimer

This is pre-release software. Features, data structures, and this policy may change. If we make a meaningful change, we'll update the date above.
