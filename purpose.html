Application information

What VISION does.

VISION is a proof-based self-improvement app that helps users turn a personal goal into consistent daily action.

Users choose a goal, receive personalised daily tasks, complete those tasks, submit proof of completed work, and track progress through points, streaks, and a rank.

How it works

  • Set a goal.
  • Receive a daily action plan.
  • Complete tasks.
  • Submit proof of the completed work.
  • Track progress through points and ranks.

Account sign-in

Google Sign-In is used only to create and authenticate a VISION account. VISION does not access Gmail, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or other Google data.
